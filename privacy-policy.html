RoboPot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the RoboPot mobile application.

Data Collection

RoboPot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users.

The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not send any information to our servers or to any third party.

Local Storage

The app may store the following information locally on your device only:

  • Selected ingredients
  • Priority ingredients
  • Saved recipes
  • Recent recipes

This data never leaves your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

Permissions

The app does not request any permissions that allow access to personal data.

Children’s Privacy

RoboPot does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
